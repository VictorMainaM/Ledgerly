Production batches

Record raw materials used and finished goods produced.

Production consumes the selected raw-material stock and adds the finished quantity to inventory. Material cost and additional production costs become the finished goods' inventory cost, then flow into profit & loss when sold. Materials need a known cost before they can be used.

Download & print production records

Choose a batch for its detailed record or include all batches and material costs.

Transport & export

Record each load, carrier, journey and transport cost.

Fuel and additional trip costs are automatically included in profit & loss and cash flow, based on the payment method. Do not enter the same trip costs again as separate expense transactions.

Download & print transport records

Choose one trip for its full dispatch record, or print every trip.

Documents & reports

Create print-ready records from transactions, production batches and trips.

For two-way Excel sync, start start-ledgerly-sync.cmd in the app folder, then click Sync Excel. Choose a separate workbook path for each company. Sync is manual: save Excel edits first. The workbook gets editable LEDGERLY TRANSACTIONS, LEDGERLY INVENTORY, LEDGERLY TRANSPORT, LEDGERLY PRODUCTION, and LEDGERLY OPENING BALANCES tabs; the original tabs are preserved.
Choose Print on a transaction for its invoice, receipt or voucher, or print a trip dispatch note from Transport & export. In the print dialog, select “Save as PDF” to save a PDF copy.
